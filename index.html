Split/Screen

At Split/Screen, every game is reviewed through seven core aspects of game design—the pillars that shape how we play, feel, and remember a game.

Aspects of Game Design

Mechanics

The fundamental systems and rules—what you can do, how things react, and how the game plays moment-to-moment.

Challenge

How the game tests your skills, whether through combat, puzzles, or pressure—and how fair or rewarding that challenge feels.

Level Design

The structure and flow of the game’s environments—from open worlds to tight corridors, and how they guide, reward, or surprise you.

User Interface

How clearly and intuitively the game communicates with you—through menus, HUDs, maps, and overall ease of interaction.

Narrative Design

How the story is told—not just through dialogue, but through worldbuilding, character design, and how narrative and gameplay intertwine.

Audio

Sound effects, music, voice acting—everything you hear and how it shapes emotion, tension, or atmosphere in the experience.

Game Feel

The “intangibles”—how responsive, satisfying, or immersive the game feels when you play. It’s the difference between clunky and clean, floaty and fluid.
